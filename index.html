The problem

```

A serious technical world means months of planning across dozens of interdependent builds. Most of it lives in your head, a spreadsheet, or a Discord thread — until you're three farms deep and realize you forgot to account for the iron the iron farm itself needs to get started.

Screenshot — dependency tangle / before state
```

How it works

```
01

Define your projects

Add what each build requires and what it produces. Import from the community Idea Hub or start from scratch.

02

Seam resolves the graph

Reads directly from Minecraft's game data for your version. Figures out what you need, how to get it, and in what order.

03

Execute

Track resource gathering from your phone while you play. The plan stays in sync — check things off as you go.

Screenshot — path generation output
```
